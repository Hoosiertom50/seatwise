### Low

- The Project Profile's "Requirements source of truth," "Artifact retention requirement," and "Preferred editor/link type" rows rely on reasonable spec-default substitutions (DEC-004, plus the retention default) rather than an explicit human answer, because the human reviewer hasn't been asked these specific sub-questions yet. This is intentional per the spec ("make the safest reasonable repository-consistent choice, record it... and continue" — Section 20) but is flagged here so the human can override any of the three defaults at any later stage without it being treated as a new decision.
- **Audit-independence limitation (process finding, self-reported):** this review handed the reviewer a pre-built checklist of the implementer's own claims to check, rather than asking it to derive its own picture of the repo from scratch and compare independently. The reviewer still gathered its own evidence (files, `find`, `grep`, `git check-ignore`) rather than trusting the implementer's audit draft, and it did surface one real imprecision (see Corrections made), so this is not a rubber stamp — but it is weaker independence than a true blind review. Accepted for Stage 00 because no dedicated `playwright-reviewer` subagent exists yet (spec explicitly permits "start a fresh review context, adopt an adversarial reviewer posture" as the interim measure). Recommendation for later stages with more judgment calls (04, 07, 09 especially): hand the reviewer the artifact and ask it to find problems, rather than a checklist to confirm.
